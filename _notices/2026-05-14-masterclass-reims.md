Following earlier masterclasses at the Koorbiënnale in Haarlem, Damask
returns to the Conservatoire de Reims to coach young one-voice-per-part
ensembles — sharing the quartet's approach to blend, text, and the
particular demands of chamber repertoire written for four soloists rather
than a choir.
