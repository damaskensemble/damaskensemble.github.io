Brahms's *Liebeslieder Walzer* — the most beloved songs for vocal quartet,
and part of one of Damask's earliest concerts — alongside other love songs
for vocal quartet and four-hand piano: Zachary Wadsworth's *Walzer einer
neuen Liebe*, built from material in Brahms's *Neue Liebeslieder* with
added texts on themes of same-sex love; Ned Rorem's *Four Madrigals*,
settings of fragments of Sappho; and Schumann's *Spanisches Liebeslieder*.
