*O schöne Nacht* — beautiful night! A program of Romantic quartets
exploring the sensual and mysterious possibilities of darkness: Brahms's
complete *Op. 92*, *Op. 31* and *Op. 112* quartet cycles, unusual quartets
by his younger contemporary Heinrich von Herzogenberg — one of whose
*Notturnos, Op. 22* directly inspired Brahms's *O schöne Nacht* — and, to
close, Robert Schumann's Spanish-inflected *Zigeunerleben*.

Katharine Dain, soprano · Marine Fribourg, mezzo-soprano · Guy Cutting,
tenor · Drew Santini, baritone · with Flore Merlin, piano.
