David Lang's *The Little Match Girl Passion*, in which four singers and a
collection of percussion instruments, played by the singers themselves,
tell Hans Christian Andersen's story. The concert, on the theme of
children's stories, also featured violinist Diana Tishchenko and pianist
Amadeus Wiesensee in sonatas by Fazıl Say and Beethoven.
