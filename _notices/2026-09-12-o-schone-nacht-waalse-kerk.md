---
title: "O schöne Nacht"
date: 2026-09-12
venue: "Waalse Kerk, Amsterdam"
excerpt: "Romantic quartets by Brahms, Herzogenberg and Jenner, with pianist Flore Merlin."
---

Damask performs Brahms's complete *Op. 92* and *Op. 112* quartet cycles,
alongside rarely-heard quartets by two of his contemporaries: Heinrich von
Herzogenberg, whose *Notturnos, Op. 22* directly inspired Brahms's own
*O schöne Nacht*, and Gustav Jenner, Brahms's only formal student.

With Flore Merlin, piano.
