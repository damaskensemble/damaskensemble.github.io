---

*New Love* pairs Brahms's beloved *Liebeslieder Walzer, Op. 52* — among
the most cherished works in the vocal-quartet repertoire, and part of
Damask's earliest programs — with other love-songs for the same
combination of quartet and four-hand piano, including modern works
written in dialogue with these Romantic standards.
