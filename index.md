## Since 2014

Damask has given voice to a repertoire too rarely heard: chamber works for
four solo singers, sometimes with piano, sometimes a cappella, ranging from
the Romantic quartets of Brahms and his contemporaries to new commissions
by living composers including Gregory Spears, Reiko Füting, Lewis Nielson,
and Federico Mosquera. The ensemble takes its name from the luxurious woven
textile pattern that emerged from 14th-century Damascus — a meeting point
of trade routes where materials, ideas and techniques were exchanged
freely. It's an apt image for a quartet that weaves together distinct
repertoires, traditions, and musical personalities into a single, unified
sound.
