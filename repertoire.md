Damask's core repertoire is written specifically for four solo voices —
a combination far rarer, and far more intimate, than the standard vocal
ensemble literature. The quartet moves fluidly between three strands:

### Romantic quartets with piano

The 19th-century heart of the repertoire: Brahms's *Liebeslieder Walzer*
and *Vier Quartette*, Schumann's *Spanische Liebeslieder*, and the
lesser-known but equally rewarding cycles of Heinrich von Herzogenberg
and Gustav Jenner — Brahms's only formal composition student.

### Modern and contemporary works

A cappella and instrumentally-accompanied quartets from the 20th and 21st
centuries, including music by Milhaud, Messiaen, Stravinsky, Schönberg,
Ned Rorem, and David Lang — whose *Little Match Girl Passion* the quartet
performs with each singer doubling on percussion.

### New commissions

Damask actively commissions new work for vocal quartet, having premiered
pieces by Gregory Spears, Reiko Füting, Lewis Nielson and Federico
Mosquera, and new arrangements by Matthijs van de Woerd and Raphael Fusco.
